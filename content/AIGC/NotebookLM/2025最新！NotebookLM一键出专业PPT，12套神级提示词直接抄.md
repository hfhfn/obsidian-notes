##### 【导读】NotebookLM 画PPT 超强模板提示词合集（2025最新实战版）

NotebookLM 的 Slide Decks 功能能严格基于你上传的资料（PDF、Doc、Slides、视频、网页等）一键生成专业PPT，避免幻觉。直接复制到 NotebookLM 聊天框即可使用。

1.**基础总结型（7张，通用报告）**

Generate a 7-slide presentation deck summarizing the key insights from all uploaded sources. Structure it as follows: Slide 1: Title slide with engaging title, subtitle, and a relevant image suggestion. Slides 2-5: Core content with 3-4 bullet points per slide, including data visualizations or charts where applicable. Slide 6: Key takeaways and action items. Slide 7: Q&A or next steps. Use a clean, professional template with blue tones. Include speaker notes for each slide with 1-2 minute timing. Cite sources inline.

2.**数据驱动型（10张，Excel/报表专用）**

Create a 10-slide slide deck from the uploaded spreadsheets and documents, focusing on data-driven insights. Slide 1: Overview with key metrics visualized as a dashboard image. Slides 2-7: Break down trends with bar charts, pie charts, or line graphs (suggest specific visuals). Slide 8: Challenges and opportunities. Slide 9: Recommendations with bullet points. Slide 10: Summary infographic. Style: Modern tech theme with green accents. Provide citations for all data points and speaker notes explaining the visuals.

3.**TED故事叙述型（8张，演讲神器）**

Build a narrative-driven 8-slide presentation in TED Talk style from the sources. Slide 1: Hook with a provocative question and striking image. Slides 2-6: Tell the story arc (problem, evidence, solution) with 2-3 visuals per slide, like icons or timelines. Slide 7: Call to action. Slide 8: Closing quote. Use vibrant, inspirational colors (e.g., orange and white). Include 30-60 second speaker notes per slide, optimized for 10-minute delivery. Ground everything in sources with inline citations.

4.**趣味卡通风（6张，教育/儿童/内部分享）**

Design a 6-slide deck in cartoonish Crayon Shin-chan style based on the uploaded materials. Slide 1: Fun title with character illustration. Slides 2-4: Main points with humorous icons and simple animations suggestions. Slide 5: Interactive poll or question. Slide 6: Fun recap. Colors: Playful pastels. Ensure content is accurate to sources, with citations. Add engaging speaker notes for a lively presentation.

5.**多源整合企划书（12张，提案/商业计划）**

Synthesize a comprehensive 12-slide business proposal deck from all sources (videos, PDFs, notes). Slide 1: Executive summary with infographic. Slides 2-3: Market analysis with transcribed video highlights. Slides 4-8: Strategy breakdown with timelines and SWOT charts. Slides 9-10: Budget and risks (visualize data). Slide 11: Timeline and milestones. Slide 12: Appendix with sources. Theme: Corporate elegant with navy and gold. Include detailed speaker notes, timing (15-min pitch), and inline citations.

6.**极简投资者路演（8张，10分钟Pitch）**

Create an 8-slide investor pitch deck in startup style. Slide 1: Problem with a shocking statistic image. Slide 2: Solution with product screenshot/mockup. Slide 3: Market size (TAM/SAM/SOM) as 3 circles chart. Slide 4: Business model with simple revenue flow diagram. Slide 5: Traction with key metrics timeline. Slide 6: Team with photos and one-line bios. Slide 7: Financials 3-year projection as bar chart. Slide 8: Ask + contact. Use black background with neon green/orange accents. Add concise speaker notes (15-20s per slide).

7.**教育培训课件（12张，内部培训/大学授课）**

Generate a 12-slide training/course deck in clean educational style. Slide 1: Course title + learning objectives. Slides 2-10: One core concept per slide, max 4 bullets, include 1 diagram/flowchart/example per slide. Slide 11: Case study or group activity. Slide 12: Quiz + key takeaways. Use light blue background, friendly fonts, add progress bar at bottom. Include detailed speaker notes with timing and discussion questions.

8.**季度/年度工作汇报（9张，领导最爱）**

Create a 9-slide quarterly business review deck. Slide 1: Title + date + team. Slide 2: Executive summary (4 key highlights). Slide 3: KPI dashboard (suggest 6 metrics with actual vs target). Slides 4-6: Top 3 achievements with photos/evidence. Slide 7: Challenges & solutions table. Slide 8: Next quarter priorities (Gantt chart). Slide 9: Thank you + appendix. Corporate style, navy blue + white, include source citations.

9.**产品发布会（10张，苹果风）**

Build a 10-slide product launch deck in Apple-style minimalism. Slide 1: Black background with product hero image only. Slide 2: "Introducing \[Product Name\]" big text. Slides 3-7: One killer feature per slide with stunning visual or short demo GIF suggestion. Slide 8: Pricing & availability. Slide 9: Comparison table vs competitors. Slide 10: Final call-to-action. Use lots of white space, San Francisco font, black/white theme.

10.**学术会议快讲（6张，论文转PPT）**

Convert all sources into a 6-slide academic conference presentation (poster-style slides). Slide 1: Title, authors, affiliation, QR code placeholder. Slide 2: Research question & hypothesis. Slide 3: Methodology flowchart. Slide 4: Key results with 2-3 charts/graphs. Slide 5: Discussion & implications. Slide 6: Conclusions + future work + references. Academic blue template, include high-resolution figure suggestions.

11.**营销活动复盘（11张，增长团队专用）**

Generate an 11-slide marketing campaign postmortem deck. Slide 1: Campaign overview & goals. Slide 2: Creative assets showcase. Slide 3: Channel breakdown pie chart. Slides 4-8: Performance by channel (use bar/radar charts). Slide 9: ROI calculation waterfall chart. Slide 10: Learnings & optimizations. Slide 11: Next campaign teaser. Vibrant gradient background, add real ad creatives from sources.

12.**超短竖版快讲（5张，TikTok/小红书风格）**

Create an ultra-concise 5-slide deck optimized for vertical mobile viewing (9:16 ratio). Each slide should work as a standalone TikTok/Reels frame. Slide 1: Hook question + bold stat. Slide 2: Before vs After comparison. Slide 3: 3-step solution with icons. Slide 4: Social proof (testimonials/screenshots). Slide 5: CTA with QR code. Use bold typography, emoji, high-contrast colors, maximum 10 words per slide.
